Credits & Gameplay Loop

Supermarket Elite is a complete 3D retail management simulation built with Three.js, procedural PBR textures, and real-time AI simulation.

1. Order Stock: Open the Supplier Market to order from 216 distinct products across 12 departments.

2. Stock Shelves: Pick up crates from your Warehouse or directly from the Warehouse Pallet, walk to any 3D shelf, and press E / ACT.

3. Open Store & Checkout: Click CLOSED -> OPEN at the top right. Shoppers will browse, pick items, and queue at the checkout counter. Interact with the register or hire a Cashier!
